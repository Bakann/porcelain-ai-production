# AI assisted porcelain image production

## A personal animation experiment by Bakann Dy

This project explores how to turn the poses of a short animation excerpt into porcelain doll imagery while retaining gestures, costume details and timing. This public repository documents the production process and review decisions. It does not distribute the source artwork or derived images.

## My role

I defined the visual requirements, clarified gestures, reviewed candidates, reported defects, selected usable variants and approved the pilot sequence. I also set limits for trials and asked for clear records of references, outputs and rejected versions.

Claude and Codex performed much of the technical preparation, scripting, generation and assembly under my direction. GPT Image and local ComfyUI trials were used.

## What has been completed

As of 7 October 2026, a first pilot passage using 11 selected drawings has been approved by me and assembled at 24 frames per second. A corrected reference image for a second passage has been approved; the remaining production is still in progress. The full film is still in progress.

## Three practical lessons

- **Review details at full resolution.** Hands that appeared plausible in thumbnails could contain extra or incorrectly connected fingers.
- **Make feedback specific.** Identify the area, describe the defect, point to the expected reference and protect the elements already approved.
- **Do not regenerate unchanged content unnecessarily.** Repeated drawings could reuse the selected image instead of introducing new variations.

## Questions I am exploring

How can a character remain consistent across generated poses? Which references help with difficult hand gestures? How can a local correction preserve the parts already selected? This notebook records the attempts, review decisions and remaining questions.

The CSV examples provide fictional templates for tracking batches, references and review decisions.
